“It Always Picks You”

Who goes first? Everyone put a finger on the screen, wait a moment, and it picks.

  • Pick a start player for any board or card game.
  • Pick several players, and the screen splits between the winners.
  • Split into teams, with every finger taking its team's color.
  • Rank everyone for a full turn order, and optionally run a fair draft.

Free, no ads, no sign-up, and it works offline. Made for phones and tablets.

Add it to your home screen

iPhone and iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android: open the browser menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
